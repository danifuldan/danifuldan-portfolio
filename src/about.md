# About me

I build software products. Operations is where I learned to spot the problems worth building for — years of watching smart people fight workflows that should have been structured, automated, or designed away.

I started building fixes — first as process improvements, then internal tools, then software products. The constant: I find the gap between how a system *should* work and how it *actually* does, and I build the thing that closes it.

Right now I'm building [Budget Itemizer](/work/budget-itemizer/) — a local-first desktop app that turns receipts into YNAB budget splits. It's the kind of tool I wish existed: fast, private, no subscription, no cloud dependency. It runs entirely on your machine.

## What I bring

- **Ships real software** — 0→1, working products in people's hands, not mockups
- **Owns the product call** — I decide what gets built and why, and carry the outcome
- **Finds the ignored problem** — the one people stopped noticing
- **Builds under real constraints** — privacy, performance, simplicity as defaults
